Here are some fun facts about Venus

    Venus although being the second closest planet to the sun. It is actually
    hotter than Mercury, coming in at an average temperature of 462°C.

    Venus is a very bright celestial body. Due to its heavy sulphoric atmosphere,
    it can be spotted even in daytime due to its bright orange appearance.

    The atmospheric pressure on Venus is very intense, so intense that it can practically
    crush most people. It is 90 times stronger than Earth's.

    Venus' trip around the sun only takes about 224.7 Earth days.

    Venus was first observed through a telescope by Galileo Galilei (famous astronomer).
