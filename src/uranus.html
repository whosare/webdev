Uranus!

Welcome to Uranus

    Uranus is the seventh planet from the sun, and one of the gas giants.

Here are some fun facts about Uranus

        Uranus is an ice giant, meanining it is made of mostly water, methane, and ammonia,
        which gives it its signature blue look.

        Uranus has a diameter of 31,763 miles (51,118 kilometers), making it 4 times bigger
        than Earth.

        Funnily enough, Uranus takes just 17 hours to spin on its axis, making a full day.
        However, a full year would take 84 Earth years.

        Similar to Saturn, Uranus has rings. It has a dark/grey inner ring, and a
        blue/red outer ring.

        Uranus' moons are named after Shakespeare's characters, such as Prospero,
        Cordelia, and Titania.

    Did you know that it rains diamonds inside of the planet
    Uranus? The methane gas inside Uranus separates the
