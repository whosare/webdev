Here are some fun facts about Jupiter

    Uranus is an ice giant, meanining it is made of mostly water, methane, and ammonia, which gives it its
    signature blue look.

    Uranus has a diameter of 31,763 miles (51,118 kilometers), making it 4 times bigger than Earth.

    Funnily enough, Uranus takes just 17 hours to spin on its axis, making a full day. However, a full year would take
    84 Earth years.

    Similar to Saturn, Uranus has rings. It has a dark/grey inner ring, and a blue/red outer ring.

    Uranus' moons are named after Shakespeare's characters, such as Prospero, Cordelia, and Titania.
