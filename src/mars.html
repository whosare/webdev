Mars!

Welcome to Mars

    Mars is the fourth planet from the sun, and potentially our future home? Or some might say...

Here are some fun facts about Jupiter

    Mars gets its name from the Romans from their god of war. This is because
    of Mars' reddish appearance, which is due to iron minerals in the dirt
    oxidizing, turning red.

    Mars has two moons called Phobos and Deimos, which are too small to have
    a strong gravitational pull causing them to instead have irregular shapes.

    Mars have a very large canyon system called the Valles Marineris, which is
    about 2,400 miles (3,870 kilometers).
    For reference, this is the largest canyon system in the entire solar system.

    We all know water is essential for life to happen. Mars did have water
    on it, billions of years ago.
    Although nowadays there is no water on the surface, there is water in
    the form of ice under the surface of the planet.

    Mars takes 687 earth days to orbit the sun, meaning for a year to
    happen on mars, it would take almost 2 earth years!
