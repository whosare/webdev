Mercury!

Welcome to Mercury

    Mercury is the first planet out of the solar system we have. This means it is the closest to the sun.

Here are some fun facts about mercury

    Mercury has the shorted orbit around the sun. This makes sense as it is the
    closest to the sun, making its distance
    not very much to traverse.

    The surface of Mercury is covered with craters from astroids and it has a
    very rocky environment. This gives it a
    look similar to the moon.

    Despite being so close to the sun, parts of Mercury's surface has ice on it.
    Certain craters that don't get sunlight
    don't get heated up.

See your age in Mercury years! (=88 Earth days)

    Surface temperature of Mercury ranges from -180°C to 430°C

    Mercury has a radius of 2,440 km, making it the smallest planet.
