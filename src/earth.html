Earth!

Welcome to Earth

    Earth is the third planet closest planet to the sun, and our home!

Here are some fun facts about Earth

    The name earth itself comes from old English and Gemanic origins, which contrasts
    heavily with the other planets that get their names from greek/roman gods and goddesses.

    Earth is only planet that we know of with water on its surface. This is crucial, as water is necessary to 
    sustain life.

    Earth's diameter is 7,926 miles (12,756 kilometers), making it the fifth largest planet in our solar system.

    Our planet hosts the moon, our only moon. The moon is responsible for our 
    tides and balance.

    Most people believe Earth is a round planet. It is not, Earth is known as 
    an oblate spheroid. This is due for a number of reasons, some of which is 
    mass distribution and gravitational pressures.

Trash is harmful to the planet, always clean up!
