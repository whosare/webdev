Jupiter!

Welcome to Jupiter

    Neptune is the final and farthest planet from the sun

Here are some fun facts about Jupiter

    Neptune has a diameter of 30,775 miles (49,528 kilometers), similarly to Uranus, it is 4 times wider than Earth.

    Also similar to other planets, a day on Neptune only takes 16 hours, meaning 16 hours for it to complete a spin
    on its axis. But a year on Neptune is 165 Earth years!

    Similarly to other planets, Neptune is named after the Roman god of the sea. Which makes sense, it is very blue
    and full of water (among other gases).

    Neptune has moons like other planets, 16 to be exact. One of them called Triton, orbits in the opposite direction
    which contrasts other moons!

    Neptune has a radius of only 24,764 km, making it the smallest of the gas giants (previous few planets are all
    gas giants).
