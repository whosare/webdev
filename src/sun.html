The Sun!

Welcome to the Sun

    The Sun is the main star that all other planets in the solar system orbit around.

Here are some fun facts about the Sun

    The center of our beautiful solar system holds our Sun. It is
    the star that each planet orbits around. It is also the 
    biggest object in our solar system, coming in at a 
    diameter of 865,000 miles.

    The hottest of the sun, is its core (which is to be expected, 
    since most planets' cores are hotter than the outer shells). 
    The temperature there is  27 million °F!

    The gases present on the Sun are a combination of
    Hydrogen (majority), helium, and a small % is made
    up of various other gases/metals.

   <-- Close up of the Sun's surface

    Similar to the other planets in our solar system, the
    sun rotates on its own axis, taking 25-35 earth days#
    for a full rotation.

    The Sun will not last forever, it has been around for
    roughly 4.5 billion years, and the hydrogen it burns will
    run out, ending the sun in 5 billion years.
