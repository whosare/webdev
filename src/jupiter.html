Jupiter!

Welcome to Jupiter

    Jupiter is the fifth planet from the sun, and the largest planet in the solar system.

Here are some fun facts about Jupiter

    The most fascinating thing about Jupiter is its extreme storms. The giant red spot you see in Jupiter
    through pictures is a giant storm that has been going on for hundreds of years! Oh, and its
    bigger than Earth!

    Jupiter, being the fifth planet from the sun, is 484 million miles (778 million kilometers) away from
    the sun.

    Jupiter has 115 moons! 4 of the biggest which are Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto. Jupiter was also first
    observed by Galileo in 1610!

    Jupiter is the first gas giant we have, meaning there is no land for a spacecraft to land onto. Jupiter is
    mainly comprised of gas and liquids.

    Despite its massive size and stature, Jupiter spins very rapidly, On its axis, it takes roughly 10 hours to
    complete a full rotation, compared to Earth's 24 hours.
