Saturn!

Welcome to Saturn

    Saturn is the sixth planet from the sun, and the second largest next to Jupiter.

Here are some fun facts about Saturn

    The most fascinating thing about Saturn, is its rings. Many other celestial bodies have rings as well,
    but none are as massive and cool as Saturns'. The rings of Saturn are made of debris such as ice and
    rocks, and each one can vary drastically in size.

    Similar to Jupiter, Saturn is a gas giant, being made of mainly hydrogen and helium. This makes
    Saturn's atmosphere very unpredictable.

    Saturn is one of the few planets that can be seen with the naked eye, due to its brightness. Many
    ancient civilizations knew Saturn existed.

    Saturn also has the most amount of moons out of any planet in our solar system,
    coming in at 270! These are the ones we have confirmed.

    One of Saturn's moons is called Titan, which is a fitting name considering
    it's bigger than Mercury, a planet!
